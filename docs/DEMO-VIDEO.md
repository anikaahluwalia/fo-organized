# Recording the demo

Two cuts from the same run: an **unedited** take for the submission, and a short, punchy cut for X.

## Before you record (10 minutes)

1. **Files.** The demo files are in `demo-files/video/` (regenerate with `node scripts/demo/make-video-files.ts`):
   - `1 - drop these/`: two quotes and an invoice with junk names (`scan_0012.pdf`, `IMG_2041.pdf`, `Oakline invoice deposit.pdf`), a bank statement called `document (3).pdf`, an exact copy `scan_0012 (1).pdf`, a vague `notes.pdf`, and **`receipt_0457.pdf`**, a Home Depot receipt that doesn't say what it's for (the mistake).
   - `2 - after the fix, drop this/`: `receipt_0461.pdf`, the next Home Depot receipt (Kiki gets it right).
   - `3 - email this/`: `Northside invoice N-5610.pdf`, for the optional email beat.
2. **Kiki.** Open the Railway address, signed in, on **Activity**. Settings: **Watch my whole Drive**, **Text me when Kiki finds something new** on. Reconnect Google once (Settings → Google account) so its 7-day sign-in is fresh.
3. **A clean Drive.** Nothing loose at the top of My Drive (move leftovers into a folder of yours, which Kiki leaves alone). Delete an old `Projects` folder from earlier tests if you want the folders to appear fresh on camera.
4. **Screen.** Left: Chrome with two tabs, Kiki and My Drive. Right: your phone, either QuickTime (iPhone plugged in → File → New Movie Recording → choose the iPhone as the camera) or Messages on the Mac.
5. **Notifications off** (Focus → Do Not Disturb), so nothing else pops up. Record with **⌘⇧5 → Record Entire Screen**.

## The unedited take (4 to 6 minutes)

The heart of it is steps 5 to 9: Kiki gets one file wrong, one sentence from you fixes it, and next time Kiki gets it right with no help. Narration is optional; one line per step is plenty.

| # | Do | What appears | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Show My Drive: messy top level | — | "My parents' renovation paperwork: scans, duplicates, names like document (3)." |
| 2 | Drag everything from `1 - drop these` into My Drive | Files appear in Drive | "I just drop files in. I don't sort anything." |
| 3 | Switch to Kiki | Within a minute: "I have a plan." under **Needs you** | "Kiki read every file and proposes where each goes." |
| 4 | Wait for the text on the phone | Fo: *"Kiki: … to file in Flooring, Finances and Home, 1 copy to set aside… Tap to check:"* | "Fo, my assistant, texts me the plan." |
| 5 | On the phone, tap the link | The review page: every file, old name → new name, and where it goes | "Real names from what's in each document. Nothing changes until I approve." |
| 6 | Point at the receipt row | `receipt_0457.pdf` → **Home → Receipts**, *"Home: Kiki's suggestion for a receipt"* | "One mistake: that Home Depot receipt doesn't say what it's for, so Kiki guessed Home. It's for the flooring job." |
| 7 | Type in the box *"That receipt is for the flooring job."* and send | "Got it!" | "I tell Kiki in plain words." |
| 8 | Wait for the next text, tap its link | Version 2: the receipt → **Flooring → Receipts**, *"Flooring, as you asked."* | "Kiki fixed it, and only that." |
| 9 | Tap **Yes, go ahead** | "Got it! You can close this tab." | |
| 10 | Switch to Drive, refresh | `Projects/Flooring/Quotes`, `Invoices`, `Receipts`, `Finances`, renamed files, the index spreadsheet | "Done, and Kiki checked every change in Drive." |
| 11 | Wait for Fo's *"Done"* text; in Kiki, **Settings → What Kiki learned** | *"Home Depot receipts go in Flooring › Receipts."* | "And Kiki remembers." |
| 12 | Drag `receipt_0461.pdf` (from `2 - after the fix, drop this`) into My Drive | Next text: the receipt → **Flooring → Receipts**, *"…The Home Depot's other document is in Flooring."* | "Next time, Kiki just gets it right." |
| 13 | Approve it (link → Yes) | Fo: *"Done"* | |

**Optional, if time allows: email.** Email yourself `Northside invoice N-5610.pdf`. In Kiki, **Settings → Email → Check now**. Fo texts about it; reply **yes**. The file lands in Drive and is filed into Flooring → Invoices.

**Expect waiting:** each Fo text takes 30 to 60 seconds. That's fine for an unedited take: it shows nothing is staged.

**Checked beforehand:** this whole beat was run with the real Claude on these files: the receipt is first suggested for Home, *"That receipt is for the flooring job."* is read as moving it to Flooring, and the next receipt is proposed for Flooring on its own.
