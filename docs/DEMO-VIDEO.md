# Recording the demo

Two cuts from the same run: an **unedited** take for the submission, and a short, punchy cut for X.

## Before you record (10 minutes)

1. **Files.** The demo files are in `demo-files/video/` (regenerate with `node scripts/demo/make-video-files.ts`):
   - `1 - drop these/`: two quotes and an invoice with junk names (`scan_0012.pdf`, `IMG_2041.pdf`, `Oakline invoice deposit.pdf`), a bank statement called `document (3).pdf`, an exact copy `scan_0012 (1).pdf`, and a vague `notes.pdf`.
   - `2 - email this/`: `Northside invoice N-5610.pdf`, for the email beat.
2. **Kiki.** Open the Railway address, signed in, on **Activity**. Settings: **Watch my whole Drive**, **Text me when Kiki finds something new** on. Reconnect Google once (Settings → Google account) so its 7-day sign-in is fresh.
3. **A clean Drive.** Nothing loose at the top of My Drive (move leftovers into a folder of yours, which Kiki leaves alone). Delete an old `Projects` folder from earlier tests if you want the folders to appear fresh on camera.
4. **Screen.** Left: Chrome with two tabs, Kiki and My Drive. Right: your phone, either QuickTime (iPhone plugged in → File → New Movie Recording → choose the iPhone as the camera) or Messages on the Mac.
5. **Notifications off** (Focus → Do Not Disturb), so nothing else pops up. Record with **⌘⇧5 → Record Entire Screen**.

## The unedited take (3 to 5 minutes)

Narration is optional; one line per step is plenty.

| # | Do | What appears | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Show My Drive: messy top level | — | "My parents' renovation paperwork: scans, duplicates, names like document (3)." |
| 2 | Drag everything from `1 - drop these` into My Drive | Files appear in Drive | "I just drop files in. I don't sort anything." |
| 3 | Switch to Kiki | Within a minute: "I have a plan." and the plan under **Needs you** | "Kiki read every file and proposes where each goes." |
| 4 | Click **View details** | One row per change: old name → new name, folder, amount; the copy set aside; Finances for the bank statement | "Real names from what's in the documents. Nothing changes until I approve." |
| 5 | Wait for the text on the phone | Fo: *"Kiki: 5 new files for Flooring and Finances, 1 copy to set aside… Reply yes, or tap to change it:"* | "Fo, my assistant, texts me the plan." |
| 6 | On the phone, tap the link | The review page, same plan | |
| 7 | Tap **Yes, go ahead** | "Got it! You can close this tab." | "I approve from my phone." |
| 8 | Switch to Drive, refresh | `Projects/Flooring/Quotes`, `Invoices`, `Finances`, renamed files, the index spreadsheet | "Kiki did it, and checked every change in Drive." |
| 9 | Wait for the text | Fo: *"Done! …"* | |
| 10 | Kiki → **Recent** | "Verified in Drive" | |

**Optional beats, if time allows:**
- **A change:** at step 7, tap **Request a change** instead and type *"put the bank statement in Banking"*. Kiki makes version 2 and Fo texts it. Approve that one.
- **Email:** email yourself `Northside invoice N-5610.pdf`. In Kiki, **Settings → Email → Check now**. Fo texts about it; reply **yes**. The file lands in Drive and is filed into Flooring/Invoices.

**Expect waiting:** each Fo text takes 30 to 60 seconds. That's fine for an unedited take: it shows nothing is staged.
