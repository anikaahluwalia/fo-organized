About this side panel

This panel shows what Kiki is doing and lets you approve her plans. It talks only to the Kiki app running on this computer, at localhost:8787. Kiki does the organizing in Google Drive, and only after you approve.

When your current tab is a Google Drive folder, the panel reads that tab's address and title so it can offer to organize it. It doesn't read pages, files or other sites.

The pairing key stays in this browser's extension storage. To revoke it, click Unpair in the Kiki app.
